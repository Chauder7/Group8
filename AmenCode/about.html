02 — About

Bringing you high quality coffee drinks in the city.

Café Centígrado Manila is a neighborhood coffee shop on Tejeron Street in Sta. Ana. Every shot is dosed, tamped and pulled to order at our bar — then poured into a cup with the little °C on it. We open at noon and stay up late, so there's always a seat for a quick takeout, a long study session, or a 2 AM catch-up. English or Filipino, order however you like.
